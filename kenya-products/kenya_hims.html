Patient Registration & Electronic Health Records (EHR)

Register patients in seconds using their National ID or eCitizen details. Build a lifelong digital health profile — allergies, diagnoses, prescriptions, visit history — all accessible instantly to authorised clinical staff.

SHA & NHIF Claims Management

Stop losing revenue to rejected insurance claims. ZYNO HIMS auto-generates SHA and NHIF-compliant billing codes, tracks claim statuses, and flags errors before submission — so your reimbursements come through faster.

OPD & IPD Management

Manage outpatient consultations and inpatient admissions on one platform. Track bed occupancy, ward assignments, admission notes, and discharge summaries — with real-time visibility across every floor and department.

Pharmacy & Drug Inventory Management

Dispense medications, track stock levels, and manage expiry dates with built-in drug interaction alerts. ZYNO HIMS keeps your pharmacy running safely and efficiently — even during high patient volumes.

Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)

Receive lab test requests digitally, record results, and deliver reports directly to clinicians and patients. Reduce turnaround time, eliminate transcription errors, and maintain a complete lab audit trail.

Theatre & Procedure Scheduling

Schedule and manage surgical procedures, allocate theatre slots, and coordinate anaesthesia teams - all with automated notifications to the right staff at the right time.

Radiology & Imaging Management

Track imaging requests, store reports, and link radiology findings directly to the patient's clinical record. Compatible with major diagnostic equipment used across Kenyan hospitals.

Doctor & Staff Scheduling

Build duty rosters, manage specialist availability, and track locum hours across all your departments and facilities. Reduce scheduling conflicts and ensure your wards are always adequately staffed.

Clinical Billing & M-Pesa Payments

Generate itemised patient bills in KES, accept payments via M-Pesa, Equity Bank, or KCB, and issue receipts instantly. Reconcile daily collections with full transparency.

Analytics, Dashboards & MoH Reporting

Track patient volumes, disease burden, revenue trends, and clinical KPIs from one dashboard. Generate DHIS2-compatible reports for county health departments and Ministry of Health submissions in minutes.
